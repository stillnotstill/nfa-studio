The four directions

Direction One

The Storefront

Leads with the product. You see all three cans before you read a single word.

The most straightforwardly commercial of the four. A full-bleed photograph of the cans fills the first screen, and the buy button is right there. Below it, flavor cards, occasions, the apothecary, and a pack-size selector.

Direction Two

Violet Hour

One can, centered, on deep aubergine. A gallery wall rather than a shop window.

One can held up on its own rather than a room you walk into. Housekeeping changes only, current pricing and tightened copy.

Open it

Direction Three

Dark Luxe

Deep forest green and champagne gold. Evening, not daytime.

The one you picked out of the eight, kept as it was. Forest green where the last one is aubergine, and a room rather than a wall. Pricing added, and the section called The Maker is now Our Story. Nothing else changed.

Open it

Direction Four

Cream Apothecary

The website is the label. Script, gold rules, warm paper, and the product further down.

The quietest of the four and the most like the can itself. It reads as a bottled remedy from an old pharmacy rather than a beverage brand. This is the one where the apothecary line sits most naturally beside the seltzer.

What would help most

  1. Which one is Mantis? Not which is prettiest. Which one would you be happy to hand to a shop owner or print on a card.
  2. What is wrong in the one you picked? There is always something. Name it even if you cannot say why.
