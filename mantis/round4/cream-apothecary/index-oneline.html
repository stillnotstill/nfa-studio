Montville, Maine

I've been growing plants for as long as I can remember, but when I was introduced to Cannabis in 2010, I knew this plant was different.

It all started in 2019 making CBD infused salve as Christmas gifts for loved ones. That became the apothecary line: salves, oils, tea, and bath soaks. The hemp in those is certified organic. I grow that hemp myself in Mid-Coast Maine and hand make each product in small batches.

The seltzer is the newest thing we make. We make every batch in Montville with organic botanicals and broad spectrum hemp extract, and every batch is third party lab tested.
