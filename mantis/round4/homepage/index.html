Everything we make starts here.

I've been growing plants for as long as I can remember, but when I was introduced to Cannabis in 2010, I knew this plant was different.

It all started in 2019 making CBD infused salve as Christmas gifts for loved ones. That grew into the apothecary. I grow that hemp myself in Mid-Coast Maine, certified organic, in living soil, and I hand make each batch.

The seltzer is the newest thing we make. Small batches in Montville, organic botanicals, 20mg of broad spectrum CBD in every can, and a third party lab test on every batch.
