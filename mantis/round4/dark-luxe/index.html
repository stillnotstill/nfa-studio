Our Story

Katrina, and a farm in Montville.

A gardener before she was anything else. Katrina worked the soil for organic vegetables long before cannabis entered the picture, and Mantis grew out of that same patient attention to what a plant wants.

Today she runs the first female-owned, MOFGA certified organic cannabis farm in Maine. Every can, every salve, every tin is made in small batches by the hands that grew it.
