Certificate of Analysis

How to read a report

A certificate of analysis, or COA, is the lab's report on one batch. It is a couple of pages, and once you know where to look it takes about a minute to read.

  1. 01Who tested it and whenThe lab's name, the batch number, and the date the sample arrived.
  2. 02CannabinoidsHow much CBD is in a serving, and what was found for THC.
  3. 03ContaminantsEach screen is marked pass or fail. Ours pass. If one did not, the batch would not be here.
  4. 04Match it to your canEvery batch gets a number, and that number is how you find its report in the table below.

Certified Clean

What Certified Clean means

MOFGA is the Maine Organic Farmers and Gardeners Association. They certify organic farms across the state, and they run a separate program for cannabis and hemp because the federal organic program does not cover cannabis.

Certified Clean means the farm is held to organic standards: living soil, no synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, and an inspection every year. Our farm holds both certifications, and it is the standard I hold everything with our name on it to.
