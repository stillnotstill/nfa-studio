The Seltzer

What is actually in a can?

Sparkling water, organic botanicals, and 20mg of hemp extract. That is the whole idea. Three flavors: Blueberry & Lavender, Hibiscus & Lemon, and Lime & Mint. Each can is 12 fl oz, and we make it in small batches in Montville, Maine.

What makes it a functional beverage?

Sparkling water, organic botanicals, and 20mg of hemp extract from our own farm. It is made to do something, not just to taste good: calm the body and clear the head. There is no THC in it, and every batch is lab tested to prove it.

Will it make me feel high?

No. CBD is not intoxicating, and the extract we use has the THC removed. What most people notice is a calmer body and a clearer head. It is not a buzz, and you will not feel it the way you feel a drink.

Is there THC in it?

The extract we use has the THC removed, and we do not take that on faith. Every batch goes to an independent lab, and the report shows the full cannabinoid profile. You can read them yourself on the lab results page.

What does 20mg feel like?

Gentle. Twenty milligrams is a real serving, not a token amount, and most people feel settled without feeling slowed down. Give it about half an hour. If you are new to CBD, one can is the right place to start.

What are the botanicals?

Organic fruit, herbs and flowers, the ones named on the can. They are there for flavor first, and each pairing was chosen to taste good with the hemp rather than hide it. The full ingredient list is printed on every can.

Is it an alcohol alternative?

That is how a lot of people drink it. Real flavor, real bubbles, something to hold at a party or pour over ice at the end of the day, and you wake up feeling fine. Some people have one instead of a glass of wine. Some have one alongside. Both work.

CBD, Testing and the Law

Is it legal?

Yes. Hemp derived CBD has been federally legal since the 2018 Farm Bill, as long as the hemp stays under the federal THC limit. Ours does, and the lab reports show it. A few states have their own rules for CBD drinks, which is why we check before we ship to you.

How is it tested?

Every batch goes to an independent third party lab before it ships. They confirm the CBD in the can matches the 20mg on the label, check the THC, and screen for pesticides, heavy metals, residual solvents and microbes. The report for each batch is posted on our lab results page.

And, all our CBD is third party lab tested. Not just the seltzer.

What does Certified Clean mean?

MOFGA is the Maine Organic Farmers and Gardeners Association. Their Certified Clean Cannabis program holds growers to organic standards: living soil, no synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, and an inspection every year. Our farm is certified, and it is the standard I hold everything with our name on it to.

Can I drink it if I take medication?

CBD can interact with some medications, the same way grapefruit can. If you are pregnant, nursing, or take prescription medication, ask your doctor before you try it. I would rather you ask than guess.

Drinking, Ordering and Storing

How many can I drink?

Start with one and see how you feel. Plenty of people have two over an evening. There is no alcohol and no caffeine, so there is nothing to sleep off. Twenty milligrams is a good serving, and more than a few in a night is more than most people need.

How should I store it?

Like any seltzer. Cold and out of the sun. Unopened cans keep fine in a cool pantry, but the flavor is best cold, and hemp extract does not love heat or sunlight, so the fridge is the right spot. Enjoy chilled.
