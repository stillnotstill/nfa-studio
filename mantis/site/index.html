Everything we make starts here.

I was a gardener before I was anything else. I worked the soil for organic vegetables long before cannabis entered the picture, and when I was introduced to it in 2010 I knew this plant was different.

It all started in 2019 making CBD infused salve as Christmas gifts for loved ones. That grew into the apothecary. I grow that hemp myself in Mid-Coast Maine, certified organic, in living soil, and I hand make each batch.

The seltzer is the newest thing we make. Small batches, organic botanicals, 20mg of CBD in every can, zero THC, and a third party lab test on every batch.

Wonders never cease
