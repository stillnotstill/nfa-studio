My Story

I was a gardener before I was anything else.

Before any of this

I worked the soil for organic vegetables long before cannabis entered the picture. When I was introduced to it in 2010, I knew this plant was different.

How it started

In 2019 I made CBD infused salve as Christmas gifts for the people I love. That grew into the apothecary, and the apothecary grew into everything else.

How it is made

I grow our hemp myself in Mid-Coast Maine, certified organic, in living soil. I hand make each batch. The seltzer is the newest thing we make, in small batches with organic botanicals, and every batch is third party lab tested.

The standard

The first female owned, female powered Certified Clean cannabis farm in Maine.

MOFGA certifies organic farms across the state, and runs a separate program for cannabis and hemp because the federal organic program does not cover it. Our farm holds both. It is the standard I hold everything with our name on it to.
