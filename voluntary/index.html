Voluntary

A man staggers in bitten and begs his friends to kill him before he turns. One of them volunteers a little too quickly.

The first two minutes play as straight horror. Five people in a boarded up room, a rope, a chair, and a very reasonable man with a knife. Then the scene turns, and nobody in that room comes off well.

  • WhatA four minute comedy sketch, plus four shorter bits shot the same day
  • WhereA living room, an apartment parking lot, and the street. Los Angeles.
  • WhenOne day. Estimated Sunday, September 27.
  • WhoFive on camera, three behind it
